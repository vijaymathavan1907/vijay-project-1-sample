B2B RELIABILITY

Engineered for Wholesale Trade

International logistics and quality consistency are as vital as the stone itself. We focus on structural integrity at every phase.

Rigorous Quality Assurance

Every single slab undergoes standard gauge measurement, gloss validation, and structural crack scanning before loading. We ensure zero hairline cracks and strict thickness consistency (±1mm).

Heavy-Duty Export Packing

Slabs are secured in heat-treated, ISPM-15 certified wooden crates. Inside containers, we use heavy-duty steel wire lashing and timber bracing to prevent shifting during maritime transit.

Full Port Coordination

Operating directly out of Chennai Port, we coordinate all export paperwork, custom declarations, and origin certifications, providing seamless FOB or CIF deliveries to global hubs.

Wholesale Block Procurement

In addition to finished slabs, we offer raw, structural quarry blocks of varying cubic volumes, ready for direct shipping to international processing plants.
